Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ON-ALLAH ENTERPRISE collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our offices or contact us.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

ON-ALLAH ENTERPRISE (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a Sierra Leone-owned money-transfer service provider operating in Sierra Leone, Liberia and Guinea. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us when you visit our offices, make enquiries, or use our assisted transfer services.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Identification details : such as your full name, date of birth, and government-issued identification documents required to process supported money transfers.
  • Contact information : including phone number, email address, or physical address when you provide them.
  • Transaction-related information : details necessary to facilitate a transfer through supported networks (for example, recipient details and transaction reference numbers).
  • Communication records : messages or notes from enquiries you make in person, by phone, or through other contact channels.

We only collect information that is necessary to provide our services and comply with applicable requirements of the transfer networks we support.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Assist you with sending or receiving money through supported networks
  • Verify your identity where required
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Meet legal, regulatory, and network compliance obligations
  • Improve the quality of service at our offices

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information only when necessary:

  • With the supported money-transfer networks (such as Ria, Western Union, MoneyGram, WorldRemit, or Orange Money) in order to process your transaction
  • With regulatory or law-enforcement authorities when required by law
  • With professional advisers or service providers who assist us under strict confidentiality obligations

We do not share your information for unrelated marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and physical measures to protect the personal information handled at our offices. Access to customer information is limited to authorised staff who need it to perform their duties.

While we work to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We encourage you to protect your own identification documents and transaction details.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, to meet network requirements, or to comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

8. Third-Party Networks

When you use a supported network through our offices, the privacy practices of that network (Ria, Western Union, MoneyGram, WorldRemit, Orange Money, etc.) also apply. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those networks for more information about how they handle data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review the policy periodically.
