Who We Are

A Sierra Leone-owned company serving three countries

ON-ALLAH ENTERPRISE was founded in October 2020. We are a registered Sierra Leone-owned business that helps customers access money-transfer services through supported networks and channels.

We operate across Sierra Leone, Liberia and Guinea with a growing network of physical branches. Our role is straightforward: we provide local access and professional assistance so customers can send and receive money through the networks available at our offices.

We are not a bank. We do not own the transfer networks. We simply make the process clearer and more accessible for the people we serve.

Our Journey

From a single vision to a regional presence

What began in 2020 has grown into a trusted network of offices serving communities across Sierra Leone, Liberia and Guinea.

October 2020

Founded in Makeni

ON-ALLAH ENTERPRISE was established with a clear purpose: to give customers reliable local access to money-transfer services.

Expansion

Growing across Sierra Leone

The company expanded its physical presence into key cities including Bo, Kenema, Koidu, Port Loko and Freetown, bringing services closer to more communities.

Regional Reach

Operations in Liberia

Service extended into Liberia, strengthening the company’s ability to support customers moving money between the two neighbouring countries.

Regional Expansion

Operations in Guinea

The company expanded its services into Guinea, making it easier for customers to access reliable money transfer services and stay connected with family, friends and loved ones.

Today

Over 100 branches

We continue to focus on accessible service, professional assistance, and a genuine local presence in the communities we serve.
